About this tool
What this shows
An estimated range for adult standing height. The 90% prediction interval means 9 in 10 individuals with these measurements will reach an adult height within the shown range.
What this doesn't show
This is a statistical estimate, not a guarantee. Nutrition, health, training load and individual genetics influence final height in ways the model cannot fully capture.
Epstein correction
When parental heights are self-reported rather than measured, a regression correction is applied to account for the tendency to over-report. Tick per parent as needed.
