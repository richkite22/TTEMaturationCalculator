About this calculator
What this shows
An estimated range for adult height based on current measurements. The range reflects the 90% prediction interval from the Khamis-Roche method — meaning 9 in 10 people with these measurements will reach an adult height within this range.

What this doesn't show
This is not a guarantee. Nutrition, health, training load and genetics all influence final height in ways the model cannot capture. Treat this as a guide, not a target.

Method
Khamis-Roche 1995 corrigendum · 90% CI: males ±5.3 cm, females ±4.5 cm (published static values)
Epstein self-report correction available for parental heights.
